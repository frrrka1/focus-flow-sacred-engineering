language · practice

Use the words when they help you see.

Focus Flow needs precise language because a blurred word can send the whole practice sideways. These terms are working tools: simple enough to use in a charged moment, clear enough to keep Character, Code, Player, Observer, Soul, and Source from being mixed together.

working terms

Definitions for practice, not decoration.

CharacterCodePlayerSoulSourceObserverSelfDisidentificationFocusIntegration

Character

The operational level of body, state, nervous system, energy, physical limits, subconscious reactions, and consequence. It is the avatar through which life is actually lived.

Code

The operational level of mind, roles, beliefs, scripts, interpretations, protective patterns, and old programs. Code can be debugged, rewritten, rehearsed, and trained into behavior.

Player

The operational level of Soul, strategy, meaning, mission, wider perspective, and conscious choice. The Player remembers that life is bigger than the current role or reaction.

Soul

The primary deep term in Focus Flow: meaning, love, gratitude, direction, and coherence of the whole person. “Higher Self” can help as an onboarding bridge, especially when that language is familiar, but Soul stays the center.

Source / God / Architect

The background of the game metaphor: architecture, not a practice level. Focus Flow does not treat Source as a human operating position; it works through Character, Code, and Player.

Observer

A recording function. It notices what is happening and gives distance from reaction. It does not love, choose, carry mission, or replace Soul.

Self

A useful term in some parts-work systems. Focus Flow may use it as bridge language, while keeping it separate from Soul and Higher Self unless a specific context says otherwise.

Disidentification

The move from fusion to space: “this fear is here” instead of “I am fear.” It gives enough room to listen before acting.

Focus

The chosen direction of attention. In practice you notice where attention has gone, then return it to the place that serves the next honest move.

Integration

Repeating a new route until it is available when pressure returns. Integration is where insight starts to have weight in the body and visible effect in behavior.
