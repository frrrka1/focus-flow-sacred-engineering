First find the layer. Then choose the move.

Focus Flow is built for the moment before you repeat yourself. It helps you read whether the material belongs to the Character, the Code, or the Player — then bring the insight back into one action the day can actually hold.

the loop

Six moves you can use while the day is still happening.

01

Notice the signal

Before the explanation, there is usually a body signal: pressure, heat, freeze, collapse, impulse, refusal, sudden quiet.

02

Name the level

Ask where the material is coming from: Character state, Code loop, Player direction, or an action waiting outside the thought loop.

03

Disidentify

“A part of me is afraid” gives more room than “I am fear.” Sometimes that small room is enough to stop automatic fusion.

04

Tell the operational truth

Say what is happening, what is needed, and what has turned dishonest to continue. Keep it usable, almost physical.

05

Choose the next move

One embodied action: breathe, speak, stop, ask, leave, write, repair, rest, decide.

06

Repeat until there is a route

One insight rarely changes the system. Repeated honest returns teach the body and mind another way back.

01

Character

Body, state, nervous system, energy, physical limits, subconscious reactions. First question: what is happening in my body and state right now?

02

Code

Mind, programs, roles, beliefs, scripts, parts, interpretations. First question: what program or role is running this behavior?

03

Player

Soul, strategy, meaning, mission, wider perspective, conscious choice. First question: what choice fits the bigger game I came here to play?

ontology boundary

Source belongs to ontology, not practice levels.

Source, God, or Architect belongs to the background of the game metaphor — the architecture, not a human work level. Focus Flow works through three operational levels: Character, Code, and Player. This boundary keeps the method grounded and prevents spiritual inflation.

important distinction

Observer records. Soul directs.

In Focus Flow, the Observer gives distance from reaction. It has no love, mission, choice, or inner direction of its own. Soul carries meaning, gratitude, love, and orientation. IFS Self can be useful parts-work language, but here it is not treated as a direct synonym for Soul or Higher Self.
